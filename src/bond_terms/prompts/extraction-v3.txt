You read the issue document of a bond (final terms or prospectus) and extract its terms.

For each field, return:
- "valeur": the value, in the format given below, or null if the document does not state it;
- "citation": a passage copied word for word from the document that states the value, at most 200 characters, or null when "valeur" is null.

Rules:
- Use only the document. Never guess, never use outside knowledge. A value without a citation copied exactly from the document is rejected.
- If the document covers several bonds or tranches, extract the terms of the bond whose ISIN is given.
- The citation is one continuous passage of the document, copied character for character: never join pieces of different lines, never add a heading or a number taken from elsewhere, never translate or rephrase. Choose the shortest passage that contains both the name of the field and its value, for example "Fixed Rate(s) of Interest: 4.105 per cent. per annum".

Fields and formats:
- devise: ISO 4217 code of the specified currency, for example "EUR", "USD", "CNH".
- date_echeance: maturity date as "YYYY-MM-DD". If the notes are perpetual or have no fixed maturity, "perpetuelle". If the document gives a month without a day, null.
- type_taux: "fixed", "floating" or "fixe_puis_variable". Use "fixe_puis_variable" for notes that pay a fixed rate first and then a floating rate (fixed to floating, fixed/floating) and for resettable notes whose fixed rate is reset later (fixed reset, resettable).
- taux_fixe: for fixed rate notes, the fixed rate of interest in per cent, as a number such as "3.875"; for "fixe_puis_variable" notes, the initial fixed rate. Null for floating rate notes.
- reference_taux: for floating rate notes, the reference rate family in capitals: EURIBOR, ESTR, SOFR, SONIA, SARON, SORA, BBSW, JIBAR, NIBOR, STIBOR, CIBOR, HIBOR, TIBOR, CORRA, CDOR, BKBM or SWAP. An Australian Bank Bill Swap Rate is BBSW, not SWAP; a mid-swap rate is SWAP. For "fixe_puis_variable" notes, the reference of the second period (the floating rate, or the swap rate used for the reset). Null for fixed rate notes.
- duree_reference: the tenor of that reference rate: "3M" for three months, "6M", "12M", "5Y" for five years, "1D" for an overnight rate such as ESTR, SOFR or SONIA. Null for fixed rate notes.
- marge_pb: the margin over that reference rate in basis points, as a number: "+1.00 per cent" is "100", "-0.25 per cent" is "-25". For "fixe_puis_variable" notes, the margin of the second period. Null for fixed rate notes.
- valeur_nominale: the specified denomination of one note, as a plain number without separators, for example "100000". If several are given, the smallest one.
- montant: the aggregate nominal amount of the series, as a plain number without separators, for example "500000000". Not the issue price, not the tranche of a later issue.
- rang: the status of the notes: "senior" (senior preferred or unsubordinated), "senior_non_prefere" (senior non preferred), "subordonne" (subordinated, tier 2) or "subordonne_profond" (deeply or super subordinated, additional tier 1).

Answer with the JSON object only.
